About KeepFlow

KeepFlow is being built as a personal AI agent — a product designed to help people complete everyday digital work with clear permissions, review, and control.

What we’re building

Many AI tools are good at answering questions. KeepFlow is focused on the next step: helping turn goals into useful work. That can include planning, research, drafting, organizing information, and assisting with workflows through tools and services you choose to connect.

How we think about trust

Personal agents only work if people can trust them. KeepFlow’s product direction emphasizes:

  • Permission before access: connect only the tools and data you approve.
  • Review before sensitive actions: sending, sharing, purchasing, or changing important settings should require explicit confirmation.
  • Transparency: users should be able to understand what the agent did and why.

Read more on our Privacy and Security pages.
