About KeepFlow

KeepFlow is a private, single-owner personal AI agent designed to run on your desk and help one person and one household with everyday digital work.

What KeepFlow is

Most AI assistants stop at answering questions. KeepFlow is being built to take the next step: turn a goal into useful work. It can read local files you allow, drive your real browser in your own logged-in profile, and coordinate specialized skills for home desk tasks, mail, receipts, groceries, local files and messaging.

The aim is a calm, quiet assistant that handles routine digital chores in the background while keeping you informed and in control. Work is summarized in one activity thread so you can see what happened, what was staged, and what still needs your decision.

Built for one person and one household

KeepFlow is intentionally single-owner. It is not a team chat platform or a shared workspace. The agent keeps context for one household, follows your House Rules, and hands work back to you for review. That focus shapes every product decision: fewer handoffs, clearer boundaries, and no ambiguity about whose data is being used.

Trust, privacy and control

  • Local-first by design: the agent is designed to run on your computer, with local files and browser sessions staying under your control.
  • Permission before access: you choose which folders, services and browser actions KeepFlow may use.
  • A physical Human Money Gate: checkout and spending steps pause for your explicit approval before anything is placed.
  • Reviewable activity: a transparent thread records checks, decisions and handoffs between the agent's specialized skills.
  • No silent purchases: sending, sharing, purchasing and important setting changes are not treated as background chores.

Product status

KeepFlow is in early access. Desktop builds, workflow examples and the feature set shown on the homepage describe the intended product direction; availability and integrations may change as the product evolves. There is no claim that every illustrated capability is available in every environment today.
