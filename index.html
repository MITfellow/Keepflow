What is KeepFlow

An agent that helps complete work, not just answer questions

KeepFlow is a personal AI agent designed to understand your goal, break it into steps, and help carry work through — from planning and research to drafts and tool-assisted tasks.

Unlike a chat-only assistant, KeepFlow is being built around execution with control: you decide what it can access, and it asks before taking sensitive actions.

What it can do

Practical help for everyday digital work

Plan and organize

Turn a goal into clear steps, checklists, schedules, and follow-ups you can review.

Research and compare

Gather options, summarize tradeoffs, and prepare decisions with the context you need.

Draft and prepare

Create messages, notes, and documents in your direction — ready for your approval.

Work with approved tools

Assist with workflows across services you connect, within the permissions you grant.

Privacy & control

Built to keep you in charge

A personal agent should be useful without being presumptuous. KeepFlow’s design centers on permission, review, and transparency.

  • Permissioned access. Connect only the tools and data you approve.
  • Approval for sensitive actions. Sending, sharing, purchasing, or changing important settings should require your confirmation.
  • Clear boundaries. Capabilities and integrations depend on what you enable and what’s available in early access.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

What is KeepFlow?

KeepFlow is a personal AI agent being built to help with everyday digital tasks — planning, research, drafting, and tool-assisted workflows — with your review and permission for sensitive actions.

Will KeepFlow act without my permission?

Sensitive actions such as sending messages, sharing data, or making purchases are designed to require your explicit confirmation before execution.

Is KeepFlow available now?

KeepFlow is in early access. Join the waitlist to be notified when onboarding opens. Features and integrations may change during development.

How is my data handled?

See our Privacy Policy for how website and waitlist information is handled, and our Security page for product design principles. Connect only the services and data you choose to approve.
