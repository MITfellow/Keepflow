How We Designed Keepflow

When Keepflow works, it feels like magic. These are the product choices that make the experience possible.

We designed Keepflow around calm, asynchronous delegation: instead of forcing you to prompt-engineer every step, Keepflow observes context, drafts complete execution plans inside its own cloud browser, and surfaces concise approval cards only when needed.

How We Built Safety Into Keepflow

Keepflow is built for privacy, safety, and security. Learn how these principles shape every part of the product.

Every user gets a hardware-isolated Linux sandbox, zero-knowledge credential injection that prevents the model from seeing raw passwords, and cryptographically signed human-in-the-loop checkpoints before any external transaction or message leaves your account.
