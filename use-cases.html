Use cases

AI agents for everyday tasks

These examples show the kinds of everyday work KeepFlow is being designed to help with. They are illustrative scenarios, not claims about completed production features.

Illustrative example

Plan a trip

Goal: “Find flight and hotel options for a weekend trip under my budget.”

  • Collect preferences, dates, and budget constraints
  • Compare options and highlight tradeoffs
  • Prepare a shortlist for your review before any booking
Illustrative example

Handle follow-ups

Goal: “Draft replies and organize what needs attention this week.”

  • Summarize messages or notes you provide
  • Draft responses in your preferred tone
  • Ask for approval before sending anything
Illustrative example

Research a purchase

Goal: “Help me choose between a few options and explain why.”

  • Compare features, total cost, and constraints
  • Surface key pros, cons, and unknowns
  • Prepare a decision summary — no purchase without approval
Illustrative example

Organize a project

Goal: “Turn this rough idea into a plan I can act on.”

  • Break work into steps and priorities
  • Draft checklists, timelines, or documents
  • Track open questions and next actions
Honesty note: These are sample workflows to explain the product direction. KeepFlow is in early access; real integrations, permissions, and results will depend on the features you enable and approve.
