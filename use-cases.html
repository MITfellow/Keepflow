AI agents for everyday tasks

Illustrative examples of the work KeepFlow is being designed to help with. Each example ends at a human decision: review, approval, or a clear stop.

Honesty note: these are sample workflows that explain the product direction, not claims that every integration or outcome is already available. KeepFlow is in early access; real results depend on what you connect and approve.

Weekly groceries with a budget cap

Goal: "Stage a weekly grocery order under my cap." KeepFlow is designed to read your pantry list, check local receipts, compare unit prices, build a cart in your real browser and stop at the Human Money Gate before checkout. You review the total against your cap and approve or decline.

Bills and receipts

Goal: "File this week's utility bills and keep a clean record." The agent can read allowed local folders or forwarded messages, match bills to accounts, file PDFs into dated receipt folders and summarize anything unusual, such as a price jump. It does not pay a bill without a separate confirmation.

Inbox triage and reply drafts

Goal: "Show me what needs attention and draft replies." KeepFlow can summarize the messages you provide, prioritize items, draft responses in your preferred tone and place them in a review queue. Sending happens only after you approve the draft.

Local file organization

Goal: "Turn this folder into something searchable." With access to a folder you choose, the agent can group documents, name files consistently, produce a short index and prepare an export. Your files stay local to the machine rather than being pasted into a cloud chat.

Telegram pocket assistant

Goal: "Let me check in from my phone." A Telegram bridge is intended to let you ask for status, approve a staged action or get a short summary while the main agent runs on your desk. Approvals remain explicit, even from a small screen.

Price and routine watching

Goal: "Tell me when something changes." KeepFlow can be shown a routine once and remember the steps: watch a price, check a note, compare against a cap and report a short diff. It prepares a recommendation, not an automatic purchase.
