Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Applies to the Keepflow website and pre-launch waitlist

Keepflow (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, how it may be used, and the choices you have. Keepflow is currently presented as a pre-launch product experience; if features or data handling change, we will update this policy before or when those changes take effect.

Information you provide

When you submit a waitlist, contact, or access request form, you may provide information such as your email address, name, role or use case, and any optional message or invite code. Do not submit sensitive personal information through these forms unless we specifically request it through a secure channel.

Waitlist submissions: When connected to a live backend service (such as Supabase), the information you submit through waitlist forms is transmitted to that service so we can manage the waitlist and contact you about access. If the site is running as a static preview without a configured backend, form handling may be limited to demonstration behavior.

Information collected automatically

When this site is hosted, standard technical information may be processed by the hosting provider or infrastructure you use (for example IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested, and timestamps) for security, reliability, and basic analytics. This page also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical requests from your browser when the page loads.

How information may be used

  • To operate the waitlist and communicate about early access, product updates, or onboarding when you have asked to be contacted.
  • To respond to questions, support requests, or security reports.
  • To protect the site, prevent abuse, and improve performance and content.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate the website or waitlist (such as hosting, database/email delivery, analytics, or customer support tools), only as needed for those services and subject to appropriate protections. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition.

Retention and your choices

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request access, correction, or deletion of waitlist information by contacting us. You can unsubscribe from non-essential emails using the instructions in those messages where applicable.

Children

Keepflow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this site.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: hello@keepflow.app (replace with your monitored privacy contact before production launch).
