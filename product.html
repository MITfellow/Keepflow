Personal AI agent capabilities

KeepFlow is being designed as a personal AI agent that can help you plan, research, draft, and complete everyday digital tasks — with clear permissions and your approval before sensitive actions.

Research & compare

Gather information, summarize options, and prepare comparisons so you can decide faster.

  • Travel, product, and service comparisons
  • Summaries with sources when available
  • Structured options, tradeoffs, and next steps

Draft & organize

Turn rough input into usable drafts, plans, checklists, and follow-ups.

  • Emails, messages, and document drafts
  • Schedules, reminders, and task breakdowns
  • Notes organized into clear action items

Assist with tools

When connected to services you approve, KeepFlow can help complete workflows across apps.

  • Use approved integrations and web tools
  • Prepare actions for your review
  • Ask before sending, purchasing, or sharing
Pre-launch note: KeepFlow is in early access development. Specific capabilities, integrations, and availability may change. Sensitive actions are intended to require your explicit permission before execution.
