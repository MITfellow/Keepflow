Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the Keepflow website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and SDKs. They can help sites function, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve security.

How this site may use them

  • Essential: Required for core site functionality, security, and load balancing when hosted with a production platform.
  • Preferences: May remember choices you make, such as interface settings, if such features are enabled.
  • Analytics: May help us understand aggregate usage and improve the site if analytics tools are configured by the site operator.
  • Third-party: Embedded or external resources (such as fonts loaded from Google, or scripts loaded from a CDN) may involve requests to third parties under their own policies.

Your controls

You can control cookies through your browser settings and device controls. Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality. Where required by law, we will request consent before setting non-essential cookies.
