How it works

How personal AI agents work in KeepFlow

KeepFlow is designed around a simple loop: you give a goal, the agent plans and works with approved tools, and you stay in control of sensitive actions.

1

You set the goal

Tell KeepFlow what you need done — for example, plan a trip, compare options, draft a response, or organize a set of tasks. You can add constraints like budget, timing, tone, or preferences.

2

KeepFlow plans the work

The agent breaks the goal into steps, identifies what information or tools are needed, and prepares a clear path. When something is uncertain, it should ask instead of guessing.

3

It uses approved tools and services

With your permission, KeepFlow can use connected apps or web tools to gather information, prepare drafts, or assist with workflows. Access is intended to be limited to what you approve.

4

You review before sensitive actions

Actions like sending messages, sharing data, making purchases, or changing important settings are designed to require your explicit confirmation before they happen.

5

You get the result and the record

KeepFlow returns the outcome — a draft, plan, comparison, summary, or completed step — along with context so you can understand what was done and what remains.

Planned experience: Details may evolve during early access. KeepFlow’s product design prioritizes user review, permissions, and transparency for important actions.
